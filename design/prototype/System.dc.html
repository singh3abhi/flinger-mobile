Flinger · Design system v0.2 · Cream and ember

Warm, calm, and easy to read

Your orange, softened. Cream and warm white carry the screens; the accent orange is reserved for buttons, hearts, badges and progress, so nothing glows at full saturation. Every value below becomes a token in tokens.ts in the mobile app.

Color

accent#E4572EButtons, hearts, badges, progress, active ring. Always carries ink text (4.7:1). White on it is 3.7:1, so it is not used.
accent-ink#B8431FOrange as small text or icons. 5.3:1 on page, 5.0:1 on cream.
accent-tint#FFE4D6Pills and banners. Pair with #7A2E14 text (7.8:1).
ink#1A1A1AText, dark buttons, selected states. 16.9:1 on page.
cream#FFF3EAOnboarding backgrounds and prompt cards. Ink on cream: 16.0:1.
page#FFFBF8Main app background, sheets, dialogs.
surface#F6EEE8Cards, received chat bubbles, chips. Ink on it: 15.2:1.
line#EFE3DADividers and hairlines (decorative). Progress track: #F0DDD0.
control#8F7F72Borders of form fields and off-state switches. 3.8:1 on page.
text-2#3A3A40Secondary text. 11.0:1 on page.
text-3#4A4A52Captions. 8.5:1 on page, 7.7:1 on surface.
text-muted#5C5C63Hints and timestamps. 6.5:1 on page, 5.8:1 on surface.
danger#B3261EBlock, delete, errors. 6.4:1 on page.
success#1B6B3AVerified email, saved states. 6.4:1 on page.
verified#1D5FD1Verified badge on profiles. 5.7:1 on page.

Spacing, radius, elevation, motion

Spacing scale (px)

4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 40. Screen edge padding is 20 (landing 16). Minimum tap target 44 × 44.

Radius

10 · thumbs
14 · buttons, fields

Pill

Elevation

  • Hard shadow0 4 0 ink, on text fields and the comment box (the Flinger signature)
  • Button soft0 4 4 rgba(0,0,0,.2), accent buttons and back arrows
  • Floating0 4 14 rgba(0,0,0,.2), like and pass buttons
  • Scrimrgba(0,0,0,.55) behind sheets and dialogs

Motion

  • HeartbeatSplash logo, 1.2 s loop: 1 → 0.9 → 1.05 → 1
  • StepsOnboarding page change 300 ms ease-out
  • Sheets250 ms ease-out; respect Reduce Motion

Accessibility rules baked in

  • ContrastText 4.5:1 or better everywhere. Accent fills always carry ink text; white text is not used on the accent.
  • Non-textField borders and off switches use the control color (3.8:1). Selected states use ink fills.
  • Tap targets44 × 44 minimum, including icon-only buttons.
  • LabelsEvery icon-only button has an accessibility label. Switches expose role and state.
  • Text sizeLayouts hold at 130% font size; display text is capped.
  • MotionVideo and loops pause when Reduce Motion is on.
  • IconsLine icons only, 2 px stroke, round caps. No emoji.
